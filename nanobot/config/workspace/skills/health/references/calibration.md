# Health Metric Calibration

Use these ranges to interpret the user's Garmin metrics. Don't just report numbers — say what they mean.

## Sleep

### Sleep Score
| Range | Rating | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 80-100 | Excellent | Well recovered |
| 70-79 | Good | Adequate recovery |
| 60-69 | Fair | May feel somewhat tired |
| 0-59 | Poor | Likely fatigued |

### Duration
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 7-9h | Optimal |
| 6-7h | Minimally adequate |
| <6h | Sleep deprived |

### Deep Sleep %
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 20%+ | Excellent physical recovery |
| 15-20% | Good |
| <15% | Low, may affect physical recovery |

### REM Sleep %
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 20%+ | Excellent cognitive recovery |
| 15-20% | Good |
| <15% | Low, may affect memory and mood |

### Awake Count
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 0-2 | Minimal disruptions |
| 3-4 | Some disruptions, normal |
| 5+ | Fragmented sleep |

### Sleep Stress
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 0-15 | Very restful |
| 16-25 | Calm |
| 26-40 | Some stress during sleep |
| 41+ | High stress, affects recovery |

## Recovery

### Training Readiness
| Range | Rating | Advice |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 80-100 | Prime | Ready for high intensity |
| 60-79 | Ready | Moderate training OK |
| 40-59 | Fair | Consider lighter training |
| 0-39 | Low | Focus on recovery |

### Body Battery
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 70-100 | High energy |
| 40-69 | Moderate |
| 20-39 | Low, consider rest |
| 0-19 | Depleted, prioritize rest |

### Body Battery at Wake
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 80-100 | Excellent overnight recovery |
| 60-79 | Good recovery |
| 40-59 | Incomplete recovery |
| 0-39 | Poor recovery, may need rest day |

### HRV (baseline ~50ms)
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 60+ ms | High, good recovery |
| 40-59 ms | Normal |
| <40 ms | Low, stress or fatigue |

### Resting HR (baseline ~49bpm)
| Range | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| ≤50 bpm | Excellent fitness |
| 51-60 | Good |
| 61-70 | Average |
| 71+ | Elevated, may indicate stress/illness |
